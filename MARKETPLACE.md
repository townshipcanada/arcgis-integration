# ArcGIS Marketplace Listing

## App Name

Township Canada — DLS/NTS Legal Land Description Converter

## Short Description (140 chars)

Convert Canadian legal land descriptions (DLS, NTS, Geographic Townships) to GPS coordinates in ArcGIS Pro and ArcGIS Online.

## Detailed Description

Township Canada brings Canadian legal land description conversion directly into your ArcGIS workflows. No more switching between tools — search by DLS, NTS, or Geographic Township right from ArcGIS Pro's search bar or an ArcGIS Online web map.

**ArcGIS Pro toolbox:**

Search for any Canadian legal land description from the Locate pane. Batch convert an entire table of descriptions into a point feature class with optional boundary polygons. Reverse geocode any point to find its legal land description.

**ArcGIS Experience Builder widget:**

Add a DLS/NTS converter to any ArcGIS Online web experience. Type a legal land description and see the result plotted on the map with section boundaries. Click anywhere on the map to find the legal description at that location.

**Survey123 integration:**

Connect field data collection forms to Township Canada via webhooks. Field workers enter legal land descriptions; coordinates are automatically resolved and written to the feature layer.

**All Canadian survey systems supported:**

- DLS (Dominion Land Survey) — Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba
- NTS (National Topographic System) — British Columbia
- Geographic Townships — Ontario
- LSD (Legal Subdivision), UWI (Unique Well Identifier), River Lots

**Who uses Township Canada:**

Landmen, geologists, agronomists, pipeline inspectors, insurance adjusters, real estate professionals, surveyors, and anyone who works with Canadian legal land descriptions in ArcGIS.

**Why Township Canada:**

- Only DLS/NTS locator for ArcGIS — no native Esri support for Canadian legal land descriptions
- 8+ years operating, serving thousands of professionals across Western Canada
- Sub-second conversion times with 99.9% uptime
- Boundary polygons included — see exact section/quarter-section outlines on the map
- Works with ArcGIS Pro 3.2+ and ArcGIS Online
